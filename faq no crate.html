Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

01 What do you offer?

We provide professional DJ and sound services for weddings, private parties, corporate events, celebrations, and other special events.

02 How does booking work?

Get in touch with us with your event date, location, event type, and any other details you have. We'll discuss your needs, availability, and build the right setup for your event.

03 Can I pick the music?

Absolutely. You can provide specific songs, artists, genres, playlists, or a do-not-play list. We work with you to create the right soundtrack for your event.

04 Do you travel?

Yes. We can provide DJ and sound services for events throughout the surrounding area. Travel requirements may vary depending on your event location.

05 What do you provide?

We can provide professional speakers, microphones, DJ equipment, lighting, and other event-specific equipment depending on your needs.
