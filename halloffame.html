All Around Sound Hall of Fame

Est. Rochester, NY - a nod to the couples, families, and events that have shaped All Around Sound since 1997.

★
Past · Present · Always
★

For 30 years, All Around Sound has been the premiere entertainment choice in the Western New York area. Created by Stuart and Jodi Schollnick, All Around Sound is a company that has the experience to make any gathering a celebration. By recruiting an energized staff, and having a great music library, along with fun photo booths, prizes, karaoke and more, All Around Sound has always been dedicated to starting the party!

THE FOUNDERS

The people behind the sound.

ALL AROUND SOUND · EST. 1997
01
FOUNDER

Stuart Schollnick

Stu founded All Around Sound in 1997, building it from the ground up. His commitment to creating memorable experiences have shaped the foundation of what All Around Sound is today. His 30 years of hard work and love for music continue to inspire all of us. We’re proud to carry forward the business he built while continuing to grow it into its next chapter. Outside of DJing, Stu’s passion for music never stops, he can often be found at local jam sessions. And if you’re around town, you might even catch him cruising around in his green ’70s dune buggy. Thank you Stu, for all you have done for All Around Sound your work, dedication and passion for the company and community is appreciated far beyond words, we are proud to continue your legacy.

ALL AROUND SOUND · EST. 1997
02
FOUNDER

Jodi Schollnick

Jodi has been a part of All Around Sound since the very beginning. As Stu’s wife and one of his greatest sources of encouragement, she helped turn his passion for DJing into the business it has become today. Back in the 2000s, Jodi helped organize the expansion of All Around Sound by facilitating the addition of our Photo Booth experience. This idea of hers would go on to help shape the company’s variety of services. For more than 30 years, she has supported All Around Sound with dedication, creativity, and care. Her contributions have helped define the company’s history and will remain an important part of its legacy. Thank you, Jodi, for being such an important part of All Around Sound’s story.
