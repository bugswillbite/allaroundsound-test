Every event is personalized around your music, your guests, and your atmosphere. If there's a song you want to hear, just ask - we take requests before and during the event.

The people behind the party.

Meet the team.

Sam Agronick

Owner, DJ

Sam is an experienced DJ and business owner who has spent years performing at events throughout Upstate New York, including weddings, Bar and Bat Mitzvahs, school events, private parties, and more. Working with a wide range of crowds and age groups has given him a strong ability to read a room and adapt to different audiences.

Read moreRead less

Music production has been a lifelong passion, giving Sam a deep understanding of how songs are written, arranged, and brought together. With an extensive multi-genre music library and experience teaching music production, he brings both technical knowledge and creativity to his DJ sets. Rather than simply playing songs, Sam focuses on understanding how each track works together to create a smooth, engaging experience for the crowd.

His production background extends beyond the DJ booth. Sam created and hosted a school-based podcast with more than 100 episodes, developing strong skills in communication, scheduling, organization, and technical production. His experience in stand-up comedy has further strengthened his ability to think quickly, read a crowd, and connect with an audience.

In July 2026, Sam became the proud owner of All Around Sound, beginning a new chapter for a company with a longstanding presence in Upstate New York. After spending four years working with the company and learning the business from the inside, he now brings his own perspective and ideas to its continued growth and future.

Nathan Brown

DJ

Nathan, professionally known as The Prophitt, is a producer, engineer, and DJ based in Upstate New York. With more than a decade of experience, he began DJing at just eight years old and has since performed at hundreds of venues across the country, including S.O.B.’s in New York City, Millennium Park, and Schubas in Chicago.

Read moreRead less

After moving to Chicago at 18, Nathan became deeply immersed in the city’s hip-hop community, establishing himself as a versatile and technically skilled DJ, producer, and engineer. His work is rooted in a deep appreciation for music history, crate digging, and the art of blending sounds and eras into a cohesive experience.

Now operating from his creative studio in Upstate New York, Nathan continues to develop his practice across DJing, production, and sound engineering. His latest projects are the result of more than a year of intensive crate digging through obscure vinyl, VHS tapes, DVDs, and digital archives. Nathan’s projects reflects his commitment to experimentation, storytelling, and the enduring power of music.

Whether behind the board or in the studio, Nathan brings technical precision, deep musical knowledge, and a genuine passion for creating memorable experiences to every project and event.

Emir Yilmaz

Photographer, Videographer

Emir is a college student, photographer, and videographer with a passion for capturing people, brands, events, and creative projects through both digital photography and video. Through EYZ Cameras, his camera resale and creative services business, he provides photography and videography services for individuals, businesses, and events.

Read moreRead less

He has worked with clients and organizations including All Around Sound, Pawsitive Cat Cafe, and Morbid Market, creating visual content tailored to each client’s style, vision, and purpose. He primarily works with a Sony A7 III for digital photography and video, as well as a Canon GL2 for its distinctive camcorder look and more stylized projects.

Photography and videography began as a hobby and have since grown into a genuine passion that he continues to pursue alongside college. He has more than 10 years of experience with creative editing software, having grown up learning and experimenting with Adobe Photoshop, Lightroom, Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Vegas Pro from a young age. Throughout the years, he has developed experience in photo editing, color correction, video editing, motion graphics, and post-production while continuously developing his skills behind the camera.

He enjoys combining modern digital photography with vintage camera styles and creative editing techniques to give each project its own distinct look. Rather than taking the same approach to every shoot, he focuses on adapting his style and workflow to fit the subject, client, and overall vision of each project.
