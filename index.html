Nearly three decades of packed dance floors.

Since 1997 we've been providing professional entertainment for weddings, private events, corporate functions, and special occasions across Upstate New York. What started as a DJ company has grown into a complete entertainment package - DJ, Photo Booth, Karaoke, Photography, and Videography - so you can build the exact night you want with one team.

Every event is personalized around your music, your guests, and your atmosphere. If there's a song you want to hear, just ask - we take requests before and during the event.
